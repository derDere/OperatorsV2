# Anchor

[Operator Reference](../index.md) · Category: **Organisation**

The Anchor is the **bookmark** for the canvas: Just like a bookmark
makes a page in a book findable again, the Anchor marks a spot on the
canvas — and automatically shows up in the editor's 📘 bookmarks
window. Clicking its entry there jumps the view straight to that spot.
For large circuits that grow far beyond the screen, this saves a lot of
dragging the view back and forth.

You set the anchor's title in the Properties window (field **Title**);
it appears right on the canvas. Small guide lines show in advance
roughly which part of the canvas will be visible when you jump there —
the anchor itself ends up at the bottom left corner of that view.

## Pins

None — the Anchor is only there to help you find a spot again. There is
no live demo here: its effect only shows up together with the editor's
📘 bookmarks window, not on a single wiki page.

## How to use it

1. Place an **Anchor** via double-click (category *Organisation*), best at
   the top left of the area it should mark.
2. Give it a **Title** in the Properties that instantly says what it
   is — “arithmetic unit”, “displays”, “input” …
3. Click the entry in the 📘 bookmarks window (bottom right): The view
   jumps there. Anchors with the same name are numbered automatically.

## Usage ideas

- **Area overview**: Every part of the circuit that belongs together gets
  its anchor — the bookmarks window becomes a table of contents.
- **Quickly switching between building and display corners**: One anchor
  at the input controls, one at the arithmetic unit — one click is enough.
- **Thinking of others**: Whoever shares an example circuit places anchors
  on the sights worth seeing.
